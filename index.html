Mazefire – Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how the mobile game Mazefire (the "app") handles information. The app is published by an independent developer. Contact: poratsoftware@gmail.com.

Information kept on your device

Your high score, today's daily-maze best, your settings (sound, music, vibration) and a saved game you can continue are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can be used to estimate your approximate location), device and app information, diagnostic information such as crash and performance data, and how you interact with ads. Where you have agreed, ads may be personalised. Google's use of this information is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted ads. Where these choices apply, you can change them at any time in Settings > PRIVACY OPTIONS. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games, the app sends your scores to Google Play Games leaderboards, where they appear with your Play Games profile name. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can control your Play Games profile and visibility in the Play Games settings.

Purchases

If the app offers in-app purchases, payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn whether a purchase is active so the app can unlock it.

Sharing

When you tap SHARE SCORE, the app creates a picture of your score and hands it to the app you choose. SHARE MAZEFIRE in Settings hands a short message with the Google Play link to the app you choose. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it.

Children

The app is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the US), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information and to object to its processing. As we do not hold personal information ourselves, requests about ads or leaderboards are best made to Google; you can also contact us at the address above and we will help.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, which is also available in the app under Settings.
